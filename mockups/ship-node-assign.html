RECOMMENDATION — Option 2 (in-panel node picker)

Map-click assignment fights the floating ship modal (exactly your bug). Keep the modal open and make Assignment → Node a button that opens a free/occupied node list. Optional secondary: “Pick on map” minimizes the modal only when requested.

  • Why not Option 1 alone? Two click targets on fleet rows (select vs edit) is easy to mis-hit; map picking still fails when zoomed out or nodes stack.
  • Why Option 2? Shows FREE / OCCUPIED + which ship, filters by resource, works with modal open, tutorial-friendly.
  • Hybrid (nice later): list is default; “Pick on map” is a footer action that closes the picker and dims the modal.
